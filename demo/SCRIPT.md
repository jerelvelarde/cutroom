# One-minute Cutroom demo

Today I joined the Gemini Argon hackathon, and I ended up building a tool called Cutroom.

It uses Argon to help me proactively edit and organize my shots, starting with the photos and videos already on my SSD.

Instead of staring at a camera roll, I can ask: what's the main story today? Argon proposes an angle, arranges the moments, and writes a visual storyboard that explains the sequence.

Then I can turn that plan into a reel. The copilot shows the tools it uses, and I can keep giving feedback while I edit.

Cutroom also normalizes iPhone HDR footage, helps match colors between shots, and renders a reviewed video locally.

Nothing is baked into my originals. I can inspect every cut, adjust the timeline, and export a new version.

This is my day at the hackathon, turned into a story, with Cutroom.
